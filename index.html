How the Odds Engine Works

Every ticket uses one uniform random draw against cumulative prize-tier probabilities, so it lands in exactly one outcome: one prize tier or no win. Presets with a published full prize structure use the operator's published winning-ticket counts. Other presets are explicitly labeled as simulator models rather than official distributions.

For a published distribution, tier probability = winning tickets ÷ issued tickets, and RTP = Σ(tier probability × prize) ÷ ticket price. Speetto 1000 and Speetto 2000 use Donghaeng Lottery's official issue tables; the displayed odds and return are calculated from those counts.
